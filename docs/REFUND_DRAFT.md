# Wedded refund policy — draft for a lawyer

Not approved for publication. Effective date, legal entity and support contact require founders and counsel approval. Mandatory consumer rights continue to apply.

## Wedded Pro

Wedded Pro is a recurring vendor subscription. Cancel any time through the subscription management flow. Cancellation stops future renewals; paid access ends at the end of the current billing period. We do not provide partial-period refunds for unused time, except where required by law. Founders must approve the process for duplicate charges, billing errors and any discretionary refunds, and supply a working support contact before launch. Checkout must clearly state the current price and renewal period.

## Vendor deposits

A deposit is paid by the family to the vendor through the vendor’s Stripe account. The vendor’s written booking, cancellation and refund agreement governs the service, subject to applicable law. Ask the vendor directly for a refund; the vendor processes it through its payment provider. Wedded records payment/refund status but does not promise a vendor refund or adjudicate the booking dispute. Your statutory rights and any payment-provider dispute rights remain available.

Agree the total price, deposit amount, event date, cancellation terms and refund conditions before paying. Keep a copy of the agreement and receipt. Founders and counsel must approve final wording and verify payment/refund and subscription cancellation behavior before publication.
