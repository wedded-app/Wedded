# Wedded terms — draft for a lawyer

Not approved for publication. Legal entity, address, contact, effective date, jurisdiction and dispute terms require founders and counsel approval.

Wedded offers wedding planning tools, vendor discovery and direct introductions. You must be at least 18, provide accurate account information and keep your account secure. Share only content you have permission to use. Do not impersonate people, publish fake reviews, harass others, upload unlawful content or misuse the service. You remain responsible for the details and permissions in photos, video, voice and messages you submit. Counsel must finalize the limited licence needed to host and display submitted content and the moderation/appeal process.

Families and vendors arrange their services directly. A listing or introduction does not guarantee availability, quality, a booking or a result. Vendors are responsible for truthful descriptions, prices, licences, insurance and performing their agreements. Families should agree scope, dates, total price, cancellation and refund terms with the vendor before paying. Founders may relay an inquiry when the vendor does not receive email.

Deposits are payments between the family and the vendor through the vendor’s Stripe account. Contact the vendor about service cancellations and deposit refunds. Stripe and applicable law may provide additional processes. Wedded’s current product takes no introduction or deposit commission; any future charges require clear disclosure. Payment processor fees and vendor charges must be disclosed accurately before payment.

Wedded Pro is an optional recurring vendor subscription. Checkout must show its price, currency, billing period and renewal terms. Cancel any time; access continues to the end of the paid period, and cancellation stops the next renewal. There are no partial-period refunds except where required by law or approved under the final refund policy. See [Refund draft](REFUND_DRAFT.md).

You can request account deletion in the app. Deletion does not automatically cancel obligations to another person; founders must verify how active subscriptions and financial records are handled before finalizing this clause. See [Privacy draft](PRIVACY_DRAFT.md) for data handling.

Founders and counsel must finalize support, reporting, service suspension, content removal, liability, warranties, governing law, dispute rights and notice of changes. This draft does not waive statutory consumer rights and is not a substitute for vendor service agreements.


## Access, content and third-party services — draft for a lawyer

Subject to these terms, you may use Wedded for its intended wedding-planning and vendor-service purposes. Do not bypass access controls, scrape private information, spam sign-in codes or misrepresent another person. Wedded and its licensors retain rights in the software and brand; you retain rights in content you submit. You grant the limited permission needed to store, process and display that content according to your sharing choices, subject to the final licence language approved by counsel. Third-party providers and vendor agreements have their own terms.

We may restrict abusive accounts and respond to unlawful content, with any notice or review process required by law. Founders and counsel must approve warranties, any lawful liability limits, suspension/appeal procedures, governing law and dispute resolution. The template's optional arbitration, class-action waivers and AI-arbitration clauses are not adopted in this draft. No statement here removes mandatory consumer rights.
