# Food photos

(`src/features/inquiry/ask-parts.tsx`): the "Veg only" and "Veg and non-veg"
cards (720 × 540) and the live counters (288 × 288; the tandoor counter
reuses the non-veg card's photo, and kulfi and chai have none). Each is a JPEG
cropped from a free Pexels photo (the [Pexels licence](https://www.pexels.com/license/)
